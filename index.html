YOUR MISSION

Complete the four Spanish challenges to earn up to 100 points. Use hints when necessary, learn the meaning of each clue, and keep track of your score.

CHALLENGE 01 · 20 POINTS

Decode the Secret Message

A folded note was discovered near the exhibition room. It contains a warning written in Spanish.

"No busques donde brillaba; busca donde se conserva."

What does the message mean in English?

"Brillaba" means "it shone". "Busca" means "look for". "Se conserva" means "is preserved".

CHALLENGE 02 · 30 POINTS

Detective Vocabulary

A detective must understand the language of an investigation. Match each Spanish word to its English meaning.

A "huella" is a mark left behind. An "alibi" explains where a suspect claims to have been.

CHALLENGE 03 · 25 POINTS

Crack the Secret Cipher

The next note was encrypted. Each letter in the message has been moved one place forward in the alphabet. Move each letter one place backward to reveal the word.

D B T B

Example: B becomes A, D becomes C. The alphabet wraps around, so A becomes Z.

The answer is a Spanish word meaning "cabin" or "hut". Shift each letter back by one.

CHALLENGE 04 · 25 POINTS

Explore Colombian Culture

The artefact's history leads us to Colombia's rich cultural heritage. Answer these questions to complete your investigation.

The Museo del Oro is in Bogotá. The Muisca are associated with the El Dorado tradition. Spanish is Colombia's official language.
